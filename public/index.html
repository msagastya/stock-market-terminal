      🏰 Economic Moat Explainer

      🏰
      ⚔️

      Popularized by Warren Buffett, an Economic Moat represents a business's capacity to maintain its competitive advantages over rivals in order to protect its long-term profits and market share.

      🛡️
      Brand Power: High consumer loyalty allows pricing power, enabling higher margins than competitors (e.g. Apple, Titan).
      🛡️
      Switching Costs: Significant time, money, or effort required for customers to change products (e.g. Oracle, TCS).
      🛡️
      Network Effects: The value of the product scales exponentially as more users join the network (e.g. UPI, Indiamart).
      🛡️
      Cost Advantage: Structural efficiencies (scale, process proprietary methods) allow lower production costs (e.g. DMart).
      🛡️
      High Barriers: Licenses, capital requirements, or patents that block new competition (e.g. CDSL, HAL).
